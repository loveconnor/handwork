# Handwork for iPhone: Support

Handwork for iPhone connects to the Handwork desktop app running on your own computer. For help, email [CONTACT EMAIL].

## Pairing your phone

1. Open Handwork on your desktop and go to Settings → Mobile.
2. Turn on mobile access and choose Pair phone.
3. In the iPhone app, tap **Scan QR code** and point the camera at the code, or copy the pairing link and tap **Paste a pairing link**.

Pairing codes expire after 15 minutes and work once. Generate a new one if yours has expired.

## Trying the app without a desktop

Tap **Try the demo** on the first screen to explore a sample workspace. To leave it, open Settings and choose **Leave demo**.

## Common problems

**"Reconnecting to desktop"**
Your computer must be awake with Handwork running. On the same Wi-Fi network this is all that is needed. Away from home, your computer needs a reachable address (a VPN address, or UDP port 38416 forwarded on your router), set in the desktop's mobile settings before pairing.

**The app asks for local network access**
This lets the phone find your computer on Wi-Fi. If you declined, turn it on in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → Handwork.

**The camera won't open to scan**
Allow camera access in iOS Settings → Handwork, or use **Paste a pairing link** instead.

**A session is waiting for approval**
Agent approvals and questions are answered on the desktop.

**I want to unpair**
In the iPhone app: Settings → Disconnect desktop. On the desktop: Settings → Mobile lists paired phones and lets you revoke them.
